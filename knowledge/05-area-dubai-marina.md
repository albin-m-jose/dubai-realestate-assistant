# Living in Dubai Marina: Area Guide

## Overview of Dubai Marina
Dubai Marina is a waterfront community in Dubai built around a man-made canal and known for its high-rise towers, marina views and busy promenade. It sits next to Jumeirah Beach Residence (JBR) and the beach. The area is popular with professionals and expats, and it is one of the six areas Marina Crest Properties covers. Marina Crest's office is also in Dubai Marina.

## Property types and sizes in Dubai Marina
Property in Dubai Marina is almost entirely apartments in high-rise towers, from studios to larger multi-bedroom units. Typical sizes are around 400 to 600 sq ft for a studio, 700 to 950 sq ft for a one-bedroom, and 1,000 to 1,600 sq ft for a two-bedroom. Views vary a lot between towers: marina, sea or city views usually affect the price.

## Illustrative buying prices in Dubai Marina
Prices in Dubai Marina typically average around AED 2,000 to AED 2,600 per sq ft, with older inland towers starting from about AED 1,400 and premium waterfront towers above AED 3,000. For example, a studio typically costs around AED 700,000 to AED 1.3 million, a one-bedroom around AED 1.1 million to AED 2.2 million, and a two-bedroom around AED 1.8 million to AED 4 million. These figures are illustrative and change with the market, the building and the view.

## Illustrative rents in Dubai Marina
Annual rents in Dubai Marina typically depend on the building, the view and whether the unit is furnished. For example, a studio typically rents for around AED 65,000 to AED 90,000 a year, a one-bedroom around AED 95,000 to AED 140,000, and a two-bedroom around AED 140,000 to AED 200,000. Premium towers rent above these ranges. These figures are illustrative and change with the market.

## Lifestyle in Dubai Marina
Dubai Marina has a busy, urban waterfront lifestyle. Marina Walk is a promenade of around 7 km that runs around the marina, lined with restaurants and cafés. Dubai Marina Mall is nearby, and JBR Beach is typically a 5 to 10 minute walk or short tram ride from most towers. Many people like the area for its dining, walking and beach access. It can be busy and noisy near the promenade, especially in the evenings and at weekends.

## Getting around Dubai Marina: metro, tram and parking
Dubai Marina has two Dubai Metro Red Line stations nearby: DMCC and Sobha Realty. The Dubai Tram has 11 stations and links Dubai Marina, JBR, Dubai Media City and Palm Jumeirah, and it connects to the metro by footbridges. Most towers have their own parking, usually reserved for residents, while public parking near Marina Walk is limited. If you have a car, ask whether a parking space is included with the unit.

## Typical commute times from Dubai Marina
Outside peak hours, typical driving times are around 15 to 20 minutes to Dubai Media City and DIFC, 20 to 25 minutes to Downtown Dubai and the Burj Khalifa, and 25 to 30 minutes to Dubai International Airport. Palm Jumeirah is typically around 10 to 15 minutes by car. Peak-hour traffic on Sheikh Zayed Road can make these journeys much longer. By metro, Downtown typically takes around 35 to 45 minutes including the tram link.

## Service charges and building amenities in Dubai Marina
Service charges in Dubai Marina typically run around AED 14 to AED 22 per sq ft per year, which is toward the higher end for Dubai. They pay for building maintenance, security and shared facilities. Many towers offer amenities such as a swimming pool, gym and security. Amenities and charges vary by building, so ask an agent for the current figure for a specific tower.

## Pros, cons and who Dubai Marina typically suits
Dubai Marina's pros are its waterfront setting, walkability, dining, beach access and public transport links. Its cons are higher prices and service charges, crowds and noise near the promenade, and traffic at peak hours. It typically suits professionals, couples and investors looking for rental properties, and it is less typical for people who want a garden or a large home.
