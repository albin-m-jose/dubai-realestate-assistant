# Marina Crest Properties: Company Profile and Service Fees

## About Marina Crest Properties in Dubai
Marina Crest Properties is a residential real estate brokerage founded in 2016 and based in Dubai Marina, Dubai, UAE. The firm has a team of 15 licensed real estate agents who help clients in English, Arabic, Hindi, Urdu and Russian. Marina Crest covers six areas: Dubai Marina, Jumeirah Lakes Towers (JLT), Downtown Dubai, Business Bay, Jumeirah Village Circle (JVC) and Palm Jumeirah. The firm works only with residential property, connecting buyers, sellers, landlords and tenants.

## Services offered by Marina Crest Properties
Marina Crest Properties offers four services:

* Resale sales of existing, ready-to-move residential properties in the areas above.
* Off-plan sales of properties in new developments that are not yet completed.
* Residential rentals for tenants and landlords.
* Introductions to independent mortgage advisors.

Initial consultations, property shortlisting and accompanied viewings are free of charge.

## Marina Crest Properties fees
The fees at Marina Crest Properties depend on the service:

* Resale purchase: the buyer pays Marina Crest 2% commission on the agreed purchase price.
* Off-plan purchase: the buyer pays Marina Crest 0% commission, because the developer pays the commission.
* Rentals: the tenant pays Marina Crest 5% of the annual rent.
* Viewings, advice and shortlisting: free.

## What Marina Crest Properties and its AI assistant can and cannot do
Marina Crest Properties matches clients with properties and coordinates transactions. The company does not give legal, tax or residency visa advice and does not approve mortgages. The AI assistant answers general questions and collects client requirements, but it does not negotiate prices or submit offers; a human agent handles those. Marina Crest does not guarantee future price growth, rental returns or that a property will stay available. For legal procedures, personal tax, residency visas or mortgage approvals, Marina Crest cannot advise on this; an agent can connect you with a qualified professional.

## Office hours and response times at Marina Crest Properties
The Marina Crest office in Dubai Marina is open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 to 19:00 Gulf Standard Time, and is closed on Sundays. The AI assistant replies 24/7. When you ask for a viewing or to speak to a person, a human agent follows up within 2 working hours during office hours. Messages sent outside office hours are answered by the assistant, and an agent follows up the next working morning.

## How to start your property search with Marina Crest Properties
Starting a search with Marina Crest Properties takes four steps:

1. Send your budget, preferred area, property type and timeline to the AI assistant at any time.
2. An agent contacts you by phone or messaging within 2 working hours (during office hours) to confirm your preferences.
3. The agent prepares a shortlist of matching properties.
4. The agent arranges viewings and goes with you.

## Property viewings with Marina Crest Properties
Viewings arranged by Marina Crest Properties are in person and by appointment, and typically last around 30 to 45 minutes per property. You can ask the AI assistant for a viewing. It records your request (property, area and preferred time) and passes it to an agent, who confirms the appointment. Buildings in Dubai often ask visitors for identification, so please bring a passport or Emirates ID.

## When the Marina Crest AI assistant hands over to a human agent
The AI assistant hands the conversation to a human agent whenever a customer:

* wants to negotiate a price or make an offer;
* has a complaint or feedback about the service;
* asks about mortgage approval or loan qualification;
* asks about UAE residency visas, golden visas or taxes;
* asks for a guarantee of price growth, rental returns or availability;
* asks something the assistant cannot answer with confidence.
